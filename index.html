Questions

Do people need an app to scan me?

No. Just their phone camera. No app, no login.

What if nobody scans me?

The back of the shirt says what it’s for, so curious people come to you. And you can always ask someone to scan it - that’s already the opener.

Are the dares safe?

Dares are done together, never at someone’s expense, and anyone can say no or spin again. Devil mode is optional, and 18+ dares are marked.

Can I play without the shirt?

Yes. With the Shirtless Pass you and your friends play from your phones for 24 hours. It’s live now: message Aron from the shop and he’ll set your group up.

How much will it cost, and when?

Nothing is on sale yet. The plan is €39 a shirt, less for groups of 3 or more. We want to bring our members quality products, and we’ll keep adding new dares, decks and features you can play with the same shirt. Join the community to hear first and get the Founders’ price.

Is every shirt the same?

No. Every shirt is made to order and printed one at a time with your own QR code. You choose which dares people get when they scan you, including ones you write yourself.

What is STREETDARE?

STREETDARE helps you break the ice with strangers

A shirt with a QR on the back. Someone scans it, you do a dare together, and you’re not strangers any more.

Why STREETDARE exists

Most of us want to meet new people, on a night out, in a park or in a new city, but the first ten seconds are the hardest. So we give you the opener: a small dare to do together.

The shirt tells everyone you’re friendly and happy to be approached, so nobody has to worry about an awkward moment. We’re starting in Madrid and Budapest.

Who’s behind STREETDARE

AronFounder · Hungarian in Madrid

Hi, I’m Aron. I’ve always been looking for games that help people actually get to know each other. I organise events and themed quiz nights for my friends, because for me the best things happen when creativity meets socialising. And when I go out, I love walking up to strangers and turning them into friends.

STREETDARE started as one shirt just for me: a QR on my back, so anyone could scan it and get a dare to do with me. Turns out I’m not the only one who wants this, so now anyone can wear one.

I’m from Hungary and live in Madrid, so I’m always going back and forth between the two. I speak Hungarian, English and Spanish.
